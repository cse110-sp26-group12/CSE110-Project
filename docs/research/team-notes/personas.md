# Personas Document

Persona: They are a design tool to help developers present their ideas and products to the represented target audience. Personas help teams understand the user goals and frustrations, and build upon that.

A persona includes:

1. The identity - who is the person using the website and their background.
2. Goals - why is the user using the website, what do they want to achieve, and what do they need?
3. Pain points - What are some obstacles that the user faces with other apps/solutions?
4. Context - How will they use this product, like what scenarios?

Project Idea: SE SitRep: A site that follows the Agile process and keeps a team of developers organized, asking how everyone is doing? The site allows teams to keep track of tasks and problems while creating a space for communication and collaboration.

## Personas for the project:

### Name: Kelly

- Context: Team lead of a team of developers, CS Major
- Goal: See who is blocked, off-track, or hasn’t checked in.
- Needs: A dashboard to track the progress of each member and issues/blockers.
- Pain Point: Wasting time asking how everything is going, need to see progress without asking, and talk to those who aren’t on track.

### Name: James

- Context: CS developer in a team that feels behind on the work
- Goal: Flag a blocker or start a task without feeling awkward
- Needs: Anonymity and low-friction tags/announcements for actions taken
- Pain Point: Hate announcing that he’s stuck on a task on Slack

### Name: Jason

- Context: He’s a commuter and works at home for most of the time.
- Goal: Can stay in the loop even if he misses in-person meetings.
- Needs: History of tasks, communication, and comments from team members.
- Pain Point: If it isn’t written down or typed somewhere, he won’t see it.

### Name: Paula

- Context: New developer, not really tech-savvy (yet)
- Goal: An interface/website that makes it easy for him to report what he is working on and ask for feedback without having to learn complex concepts or tech mechanics.
- Needs: Tooltips, tutorial, explanations in plain English, or a guide to help ask for help/report updates (daily standup)
- Pain Points: Feeling anxious—scared to change things he wasn’t supposed to or straight confused with all the buttons and texts.

These personas look at different roles within a development team:

- Team leads: people who hold a lot of responsibility, especially when it comes to organizing the project and keeping track of members’ progress.
- Team members: specifically members who would have a problem, because, of course, there are many kinds of members, but most will effectively use the site and have no problem with staying focused on the project. So we are looking at team members who may struggle with the use of the website.
- Hybrid Teammate: This is to cover the in-person vs online relationship.
- Not Really Tech Savvy: This is to cover the low and high experience levels within team dynamics.
